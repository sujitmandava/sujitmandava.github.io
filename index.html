About me

Wren

In beta

Hear how they're really doing.

A care companion for families of a parent with memory loss. Short daily voice conversations and check-ins become a calm, readable picture of mood, medications, and gentle shifts in speech. Every change is measured against the person's own baseline, never against a diagnosis, and the whole family and the doctor see the same picture.

  • Speech and language trends drawn from everyday conversation, compared only to the person's own history
  • Ask Wren: a RAG assistant that grounds GPT-4o in recent sessions, transcripts, check-ins, and notes
  • One React codebase ships to web, iOS, and Android via Capacitor

FPL Agent AI

In development

A Fantasy Premier League planner for anyone with a team ID.

Source ↗

Enter an FPL team ID and it plans your week from FPL's public data: transfers, lineup, captain, and when to play each chip. There's no login and it never touches your account; it shows the moves and you make them. The Plan screen draws them on a tactics board against your mini-league rival.

  • An expected-points model built from minutes, xG/xA, defensive contributions, and opponent-adjusted team ratings, backtested walk-forward against a points-per-game baseline
  • A mixed-integer program picks the squad, XI, and captain for every gameweek in the horizon, and gives every remaining chip its own week before it expires
  • Mini-league view: rivals' captains and chips, and effective ownership that counts captains double
  • Python
  • PuLP
  • pandas
  • FastAPI
  • Next.js

SafeWatch

MVP

Faster response to safety events in nursing homes.

In a short-staffed nursing home, a resident's fall can go unnoticed for too long. SafeWatch puts falls and sensor alerts into one triage queue and won't let them sit: every alert starts a countdown, and if nobody acknowledges it, it escalates from CNA to charge nurse to administrator.

  • SLA countdowns and role-based escalation, so no alert depends on one person noticing it
  • An immutable audit trail of every state change: who was alerted, who acknowledged, and when
  • Takes events from an API, a UI simulator, or recorded JSON replays

  • SQLAlchemy
  • PostgreSQL
  • React
